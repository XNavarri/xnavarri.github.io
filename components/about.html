About me

I am a neuroscientist with 10 years of experience applying computational methods to biological problems, which has led to 8 peer-reviewed publications, 5 of them as first author. My work sits at the intersection of genetics, epidemiology and neuroscience, using multiscale and biologically informed approaches.

I integrate high-dimensional data (genomics, transcriptomics, lipidomics and metabolomics, spatial transcriptomics, neuroimaging, cognitive and clinical data) from large population cohorts of up to 30,000+ participants, and combine genomic and phenotypic information to prioritise candidate genes and variants of interest.

As a Senior Scientist at the Barcelonaβeta Brain Research Center, I build reproducible HPC pipelines for large-scale GWAS of ~3,600 biomarkers and study how complex lipids relate to brain structure in healthy and Alzheimer's disease risk-enriched cohorts. I completed my Ph.D. in Neuroscience at the Université de Montréal with Prof. Tomáš Paus.

Research areas

  • Statistical genetics

    GWAS, polygenic scores, fine-mapping, eQTL and enrichment analyses to identify and prioritise variants and genes.

  • Population neuroscience

    Development and maturation of the human cerebral cortex across the lifespan, and how genes and environment shape it.

  • Multi-omics integration

    Lipidomics, metabolomics and (spatial and single-cell) transcriptomics linked to brain phenotypes and implicated cell types.

  • Epidemiology & meta-analysis

    Prenatal exposures and substance use, causal inference, longitudinal modelling and systematic reviews.
